### Finding 14 (major, safety): the test apps sent a synthetic Alt key

| # | Severity | Finding | Resolution | Regression test |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 14 | Major, safety (fixed on this branch) | `egui::ViewportCommand::Focus` is implemented by winit 0.30.13 as `focus_window`, which, when the window is visible, not minimized and not the foreground window, calls `force_window_active` (`src/platform_impl/windows/window.rs:1537`): a `SendInput` of the left Alt key, down and up, to obtain the foreground permission. `tools/s4/testapp/src/main.rs` (since PR #12) and `tools/s4/testeditor/src/main.rs` (since PR #19) sent the command on every frame during the first 2 s after start. So each launch of the test app (rounds after PR #12) or of the editor (after PR #19) with its window not in front sent at least one Alt tap outside the engine's gate, while the owner might be using another application. That breaks AGENTS.md rule 6 (input only through the gate) and the eighth round's "no Alt trick" rule. No evidence was found that the key reached another application, but it cannot be ruled out: the client cannot see synthetic keys, and the key goes to whichever window is in front at that moment. | The command is removed from both apps. Nothing else in either app reaches `force_window_active` (its only other caller is the creation of a fullscreen window, which neither app uses). The client's activation ladder (window management by handle, no input) is the only way to the front. The editor keeps its `FocusSeeker` (hide and show the window, no input). | `the_source_never_asks_the_toolkit_for_the_foreground` in each app: fails if the source contains the focus command, `focus_window` or a fullscreen request (checked by adding the command to a copy of each source: both fail). |

- **Cause.** The command was chosen to "ask for the focus once, early" and its input side effect was not in the apps' source. It was noticed while verifying finding 10 (see its note above) and confirmed in the winit source.
- **Consequence for finding 10.** With the Alt-tap build the editor was the foreground window with no Win32 focus window in every sample of four experimental builds. Without the command, the final builds had the UI Automation focus on the editor in every sample of three launches (two without any request, one with the client's ladder, which found it `already_foreground`), and on the test app in two launches (one without any request, one with the ladder); an earlier build of the same source had it in four of four editor launches. So the Alt-tap activation is the likely cause of finding 10; this is shown by comparing builds, not by isolating the key. `FocusSeeker` was not shown to fire in the new build; it stays as a bounded, input-free fallback.
- **Which runs are affected.** Every earlier live round that launched the test app (E2E-01 and the pilots after PR #12) or the editor (rounds with E2E-02 after PR #19: the eighth round and the ninth round and its continuation). The Alt tap was sent by the app at start, before any request of the client; it is not counted in the ledger and not in the engine's metrics.
