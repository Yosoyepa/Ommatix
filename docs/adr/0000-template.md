# NNNN. Title in imperative form

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by [NNNN](NNNN-title.md)
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Approval:** Pending | who approved it, and when

## Context

What forces are at play, and what problem needs a decision. Cite evidence: a thesis chapter,
a source (S01–S24), a measurement.

## Decision

What we will do, stated plainly.

## Alternatives considered

- **Option:** why not.

## Consequences and cost

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what we must watch.

## Required validation

The tests or measurements (by identifier) that must pass before the decision is trusted.

## Reversibility

What it would take to undo it, and what would trigger that.

## Provenance

Where the decision comes from, and which ADR it supersedes or refines.

## Resumen (ES)

Dos a cinco líneas en español con la decisión y su motivo.
